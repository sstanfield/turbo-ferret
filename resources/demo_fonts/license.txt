The fonts in this folder are made by turbo-ferret-fontgen (tools/fontgen) from
DejaVu Sans (../DejaVuSans.ttf): its glyphs rasterized into bitmap strips, with a
shadow, a glow or a color gradient baked in.

They take the place of the bitmap fonts Turbo Badger ships in its folder of the
same name, which were rasterized from fonts whose licences its author could not
vouch for ("use at your own risk"). None of those files are here.

DejaVu Sans is under the Bitstream Vera licence with the DejaVu and Arev
additions: see ../DejaVuSans-LICENSE.txt. Its notices go with every copy of the
font, these bitmaps included. As that licence asks of altered versions, none of
these fonts is called "Bitstream", "Vera" or "Arev".

To make them again:
  cargo run -p turbo-ferret-fontgen -- resources/DejaVuSans.ttf --strip shadow --sizes 14,28 --out resources/default_font/dejavu_white_with_shadow
  cargo run -p turbo-ferret-fontgen -- resources/DejaVuSans.ttf --strip glow --sizes 42 --out resources/demo_fonts/glow
  cargo run -p turbo-ferret-fontgen -- resources/DejaVuSans.ttf --strip sunset --sizes 30 --out resources/demo_fonts/sunset
  cargo run -p turbo-ferret-fontgen -- resources/DejaVuSans.ttf --strip candy --sizes 48 --out resources/demo_fonts/candy
