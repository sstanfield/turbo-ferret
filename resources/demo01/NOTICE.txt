These files come from Turbo Badger's demo (Demo/demo01 in
https://github.com/fruxo/turbobadger, by Emil Segerås): the layouts in
ui_resources, the demo skin and the images.

The files in ui_resources are altered for Turbo Ferret, the Rust port: the
texts they show name Turbo Ferret and its widgets instead of Turbo Badger's
classes and build options, a few spelling mistakes in those texts are
corrected, and image paths are relative to this resources folder. The widget
type names (TBButton, TBLayout ...) and everything else are as in the
originals.
